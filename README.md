# ⚡ Electric Vehicle Market Performance & Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Performance & Insights Dashboard** developed using Tableau. It aims to explore and analyze the electric vehicle market through key performance indicators, battery technologies, vehicle specifications, and manufacturer sales data.

The dashboard transforms raw EV market data into meaningful visual insights, helping users understand market trends, compare vehicle categories, and evaluate different aspects of electric vehicle performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze electric vehicle performance across different vehicle categories.
- Compare the average driving range of different EV categories.
- Understand the distribution of battery technologies used in electric vehicles.
- Evaluate battery efficiency and technology distribution.
- Analyze vehicle acceleration (0–100 km/h) performance.
- Explore the relationship between vehicle weight and acceleration.
- Compare annual sales performance across different manufacturers.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The project includes the following visualizations:

### 1. Manufacturer Sales Analysis
Analyzes annual electric vehicle sales across different manufacturers to understand their market performance.

### 2. Battery Technology Distribution
Visualizes the distribution of different battery technologies across electric vehicle categories.

### 3. Average Range by Category
Compares the average driving range (in kilometers) across different EV categories.

### 4. Battery Efficiency
Explores battery-related performance metrics and compares battery technologies across vehicle categories.

### 5. Acceleration Analysis (0–100)
Examines the acceleration performance of electric vehicles, highlighting differences across battery types and vehicle categories.

### 6. Vehicle Weight Analysis
Studies the weight distribution of electric vehicles and its relationship with acceleration performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau Desktop** – Data visualization and interactive dashboard development.
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data handling and preparation.
- **Data Analytics** – Exploring patterns, comparisons, and market trends.

## 💡 Key Insights & Analysis

This dashboard is designed to help explore important questions about the EV industry:

- Which manufacturers have higher annual EV sales?
- Which vehicle categories offer better driving range?
- What battery technologies are represented in the EV market?
- How does acceleration vary across electric vehicle categories?
- How do vehicle weights differ across EV segments?
- What patterns can be observed in battery technology and vehicle performance?
